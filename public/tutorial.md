Welcome to 5x5GAME, a unique puzzle experience designed to challenge your strategic thinking and problem-solving skills. Navigate through a grid and reach the goal number, 25, using strategic moves. This guide will walk you through the basics of the game, the rules, and strategies to help you succeed.


## Game Overview

In 5x5GAME, your objective is to navigate a 5x5 grid and reach the goal number, 25. You must move strategically across the grid, taking calculated leaps both vertically, horizontally, and diagonally.


## How to Play

### Starting the Game:


When you start the game, you'll see a 5x5 grid. and you need to click on an empty tile to start


### Movement Rules:

#### Vertical and Horizontal Moves:

You can move vertically or horizontally by 3 steps at a time.
For example, if you your last move is on tile 1x1, you can move to tile 1X4 or 4x1, provided these tiles are within the grid and not already visited.


### Diagonal Moves:

You can move diagonally by 2 steps at a time.
For example, if you are on tile 3x3, you can move to tile 1x1, 5x1, 1x5 or 5x5, provided these tiles are within the grid and not already visited.


### Objective:

Plan your moves to reach the goal number, 25.
Avoid landing on the same tile more than once.


## Gameplay:

Click or tap on a tile to visit it. Only valid moves will be allowed.
Think ahead and plan your path to ensure you can reach the goal.
