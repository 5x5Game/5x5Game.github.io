**Challenge your mind with a unique puzzle experience!** In 5x5GAME, navigate a grid using strategic steps to reach the goal number: 25.


## Here's what awaits you:

- **Intriguing Movement:** Take calculated leaps across the grid. 
  * Move vertically or horizontally by **3 steps** at once. 
  * Test your diagonal thinking with **2 steps** diagonal jumps.
- **Sharpen your mind:** Plan your moves carefully, considering both distance and available jumps.
- **No room for hesitation:** Once you land, the game progresses, requiring quick thinking and strategic maneuvering.
